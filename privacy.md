# Privacy Policy — Cue

**Last updated:** 27 August 2026

This describes what Cue ("the app") collects, why, and what rights you have
over it. Cue is developed by [your name/business here] ("we", "us").
Contact for anything privacy-related: [your email here].

> This document is a starting point drafted alongside the app's code, not a
> substitute for legal review — particularly before Cue accepts payments or
> is published publicly at any scale.

## What we collect

**From Google Sign-In**, when you sign in: your name, email address, and
profile photo. We don't request or receive access to your Google Drive,
Gmail, or other Google data — only basic profile info needed to identify
your account.

**Content you create**: the videos you add, the notes you write and their
timestamps, project names, and who you invite to collaborate.

**Failure logs**: if something in the app breaks, a record of the error
message, a technical stack trace, your device platform, and the app
version — tied to your account if you were signed in, so we can find and
fix what went wrong. This never includes the content of your notes.

We don't run ads, don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and
don't sell data to anyone.

## Why we process it

- **To provide the service** (Google sign-in, storing and syncing your
  notes, showing them to collaborators you've invited): this is necessary
  to fulfil what you're using Cue for.
- **To keep it reliable** (failure logs): our legitimate interest in
  finding and fixing bugs, weighed against the limited, non-content data
  involved.

## Who else sees it

- **Supabase** hosts our database, authentication, and backend functions.
  As of this writing that infrastructure runs in the United States (AWS
  `us-west-2`), so your data is transferred outside the EU/EEA; this
  happens under Supabase's standard data processing terms, which include
  Standard Contractual Clauses as the transfer safeguard.
- **Google** provides sign-in and, separately, the YouTube Data API we use
  to look up a video's title and thumbnail — Google sees the YouTube video
  IDs you add, nothing else from inside the app.
- **Collaborators you invite to a project** see that project's videos and
  notes, including ones written by other collaborators — inviting someone
  is a deliberate sharing action you take, not something that happens
  automatically.
- We do not otherwise share your data with third parties.

## How long we keep it

Your notes, projects, and profile are kept until you delete them or delete
your account. Failure logs are kept only as long as useful for debugging —
we aim not to hold these longer than about 90 days, though at this stage
that cleanup is manual rather than automatic.

## Your rights

- **Access & portability** — export any video's notes (or a whole
  project's) as a text file from inside the app at any time.
- **Correction** — edit or delete any note yourself, whenever you like.
- **Erasure** — delete your account from Account → Delete account. This
  permanently removes your profile and everything you've authored,
  including any project you own (which also removes it for anyone you'd
  invited to collaborate — you'll be warned about this before confirming).
- **Objection / restriction** — contact us at the address above and we'll
  work it out with you directly; there's no automated opt-out flow yet
  given the size of this project.
- **Complaints** — if you're in the EU/EEA, you can lodge a complaint with
  your local data protection authority (in Italy, the [Garante per la
  protezione dei dati personali](https://www.gpdp.it)) at any time.

## Children

Cue isn't directed at children and we don't knowingly collect data from
anyone under 16.

## Changes

If this policy changes meaningfully, the "Last updated" date above will
change accordingly.
