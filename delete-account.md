# Delete your Cue account

<p class="lede">You can permanently delete your Cue account and associated personal data either inside the app or by sending a request from this page. You do not need to reinstall Cue.</p>

## Delete immediately in the app

Open **Cue → Account → Delete account**, review the warning, then select **Delete everything**. The deletion cannot be undone.

## Request deletion without the app

Use the button below to email Niccolo Felicani, the developer of Cue. Send the request from the same Google email address used for your Cue account whenever possible. This helps verify that the account belongs to you.

<a class="button" href="mailto:felicani.1844775@gmail.com?subject=Cue%20account%20deletion%20request&amp;body=Please%20permanently%20delete%20the%20Cue%20account%20registered%20to%20this%20email%20address%20and%20its%20associated%20personal%20data.%0A%0ACue%20account%20email%3A%20%0A%0AOptional%20message%3A%20">Email deletion request</a>

If the button does not open your email app, send a message to [felicani.1844775@gmail.com](mailto:felicani.1844775@gmail.com) with the subject **Cue account deletion request** and include the Google email used for Cue.

We may ask for reasonable verification if the request comes from a different address. Requests are acknowledged and completed as soon as reasonably possible, normally within 30 days.

## What deletion removes

- Your Cue authentication account and stored profile information, including your name, email address, and profile photo.
- Notes you authored, project memberships, and projects you own.
- The link between your account and videos you added.
- Identifiable diagnostic records associated with your account.

Projects owned by you are removed. Notes authored by other people remain their content and return to their own library without the deleted project. Cached public YouTube metadata may remain because it is not account data. Limited residual copies may remain temporarily in infrastructure backups before they are overwritten.
